Before you book

Cost, comfort, insurance, and timing answered early

Patients should never need to call just to discover the basics.

Do you accept insurance?

Yes. The team verifies benefits before treatment and reviews out-of-pocket estimates before care begins.

Can anxious patients book a slower first visit?

Yes. Choose comfort consultation or clinic tour in the booking form and we will schedule extra time.

Do you offer same-day emergency care?

Emergency slots are held each day for pain, swelling, broken teeth, and urgent dental trauma.

Are payment plans available?

Financing and staged treatment plans are available for cosmetic, implant, and orthodontic care.
